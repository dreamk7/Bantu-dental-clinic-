Missing Teeth

📋 What is it?

Missing teeth can affect your ability to chew, speak, and smile with confidence. They can also cause remaining teeth to shift, leading to bite problems and bone loss.

⚠️ Common Causes
  • Severe tooth decay or gum disease
  • Trauma or injury to the mouth
  • Congenital conditions (missing teeth from birth)
  • Failed previous dental work
  • Advanced age
💊 How We Treat It
  • Dental Implants: Permanent, natural-looking replacement
  • Dental Bridges: Fixed restoration using adjacent teeth
  • Full Dentures: For complete tooth loss
  • Partial Dentures: For multiple missing teeth
  • Implant-Supported Dentures: For added stability
🔩 Permanent Solutions
🛡️ Prevention

Regular checkups, good oral hygiene, and prompt treatment of decay or gum disease can prevent tooth loss.

💔

Broken or Cracked Teeth

📋 What is it?

A broken or cracked tooth can range from a small chip to a severe fracture that exposes the tooth's pulp. It can cause pain, sensitivity, and further damage if left untreated.

⚠️ Common Causes
  • Trauma or injury to the mouth
  • Teeth grinding (bruxism)
  • Biting on hard objects (ice, nuts, hard candy)
  • Large fillings that weaken the tooth
  • Untreated decay
💊 How We Treat It
  • Dental Crowns: Full coverage to protect and restore function
  • Inlays & Onlays: Custom restorations for moderate damage
  • Dental Bonding: For smaller chips and cracks
  • Root Canal Therapy: If the pulp is exposed or infected
👑 Durable Crowns
🛡️ Prevention

Wear a mouthguard during sports, avoid chewing hard objects, and use a night guard if you grind your teeth.

🔄

Root Canal Infection

📋 What is it?

A root canal infection occurs when the soft tissue inside the tooth (pulp) becomes infected or inflamed. It can cause severe pain, swelling, and tooth loss if not treated promptly.

⚠️ Common Causes
  • Deep tooth decay that reaches the pulp
  • Trauma or injury to the tooth
  • Repeated dental procedures on the same tooth
  • Cracked or broken tooth
  • Untreated cavities
💊 How We Treat It
  • Root Canal Therapy: Removes infected pulp, cleans, and seals the tooth
  • Dental Crown: To protect and restore the tooth after treatment
  • Pain Management: Gentle techniques for maximum comfort
  • Antibiotics: If infection has spread
🩺 Pain-Free Treatment
🛡️ Prevention

Good oral hygiene, regular checkups, and prompt treatment of cavities can prevent root canal infections.

TMJ / TMD Disorder

📋 What is it?

TMJ disorder affects the temporomandibular joint, which connects your jaw to your skull. It can cause jaw pain, headaches, earaches, and difficulty opening or closing your mouth.

⚠️ Common Causes
  • Teeth grinding or clenching (bruxism)
  • Stress and anxiety
  • Arthritis in the jaw joint
  • Jaw injury or trauma
  • Misaligned bite
💊 How We Treat It
  • Night Guards: Custom-fitted to prevent grinding
  • Physical Therapy: Exercises to relieve tension
  • Bite Adjustment: To correct misalignment
  • Stress Management: Techniques to reduce clenching
  • Medication: For pain and inflammation
😌 Pain Relief
🛡️ Prevention

Stress management, relaxation techniques, and using a night guard can help prevent TMJ symptoms.

🦠

Severe Tooth Decay

📋 What is it?

Severe tooth decay occurs when cavities penetrate deep into the tooth, reaching the dentin or pulp. It can cause pain, infection, and eventual tooth loss if left untreated.

⚠️ Common Causes
  • Poor oral hygiene and irregular brushing
  • High-sugar diet
  • Dry mouth (reduced saliva flow)
  • Lack of regular dental checkups
  • Genetics and tooth structure
💊 How We Treat It
  • Dental Crowns: To cover and protect severely decayed teeth
  • Root Canal Therapy: If the pulp is infected
  • Large Fillings: For moderate decay
  • Extraction: If the tooth cannot be saved
🔧 Restorative Options
🛡️ Prevention

Brush twice daily, floss regularly, limit sugar intake, and visit us for routine cleanings.

😁

Denture Wearers

📋 What is it?

Denture wearers are individuals who have lost some or all of their teeth and use removable dentures to restore function and appearance. Over time, dentures may need adjustment or replacement.

⚠️ Common Causes
  • Tooth loss from decay, gum disease, or trauma
  • Advanced age
  • Medical conditions affecting oral health
  • Failing previous dental work
  • Bone loss in the jaw
💊 How We Treat It
  • Full Dentures: Complete tooth replacement
  • Partial Dentures: For multiple missing teeth
  • Implant-Supported Dentures: Added stability and comfort
  • Denture Relining: To improve fit and comfort
  • Denture Repairs: Quick fixes for broken dentures
😁 Natural Look & Feel
🛡️ Prevention

Regular dental checkups and proper denture care can extend the life of your dentures and keep your mouth healthy.
