Our Dedicated Team

We are a family of dental professionals committed to your comfort and oral health. Get to know the faces behind your smile.

Dr. Max Etemadi

Founder & Lead Dentist
What do I do?

I lead our clinical team with 18+ years of experience. I perform everything from routine cleanings to complex implant surgeries. My goal is to make every patient feel heard and cared for.

Why do I love dentistry?

I love seeing the transformation in my patients — not just their smiles, but their confidence. When someone can smile without hesitation, it changes their life. That's why I do what I do.

Fun fact about me

I'm a self-taught guitar player and I love hiking the Cascades with my family!

⭐ Top Rated

Dr. Emily Park

Associate Dentist
What do I do?

I specialize in cosmetic and restorative procedures — from veneers and whitening to crowns and bridges. I work closely with patients to design their dream smiles.

Why do I love dentistry?

I love the artistry of dentistry. Every smile is unique, and I get to be both an artist and a healer. Seeing a patient's face light up after their smile makeover is the best feeling.

Fun fact about me

I used to be a professional ballroom dancer! I competed for 10 years before going to dental school.

✨ Cosmetic Expert

Sarah Johnson

Lead Dental Hygienist
What do I do?

I provide preventive care — cleanings, oral cancer screenings, and gum disease treatments. I educate patients on home care and make sure they feel comfortable during every visit.

Why do I love dentistry?

I love helping patients build healthy habits that last a lifetime. When a patient tells me their gums feel better or they're no longer anxious about cleanings, I know I've made a difference.

Fun fact about me

I'm an avid gardener and I grow over 30 varieties of tomatoes in my backyard!

💙 Patient Favorite

Dr. James Wu

Oral Surgeon
What do I do?

I handle complex surgical cases — dental implants, wisdom teeth extractions, and bone grafting. I work with our team to ensure seamless, pain-free surgical care.

Why do I love dentistry?

I love the precision of surgery and the immediate impact it has on patients' lives. When I remove pain or place a permanent implant, I'm giving someone their quality of life back.

Fun fact about me

I'm a certified scuba diver and have explored dive sites in 7 countries!
