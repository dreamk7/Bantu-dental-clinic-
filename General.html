Tooth Decay (Cavities)

📋 What is it?

Tooth decay is the destruction of tooth enamel caused by acids produced by bacteria in plaque. It creates cavities that, if left untreated, can lead to pain, infection, and tooth loss.

⚠️ Common Causes
  • Poor oral hygiene and irregular brushing
  • Frequent consumption of sugary or acidic foods and drinks
  • Dry mouth (reduced saliva flow)
  • Deep tooth crevices that trap food and bacteria
💊 How We Treat It
  • Fillings: Tooth-colored composite fillings for small to moderate cavities
  • Fluoride Treatments: Professional fluoride varnish to strengthen enamel
  • Dental Sealants: Protective coatings for children's molars
  • Crowns: For advanced decay, we may recommend a dental crown
✅ Mercury-Free Fillings
🛡️ Prevention

Regular cleanings, fluoride treatments, and reducing sugar intake can help prevent tooth decay. We'll work with you to build healthy habits.

Gum Disease (Periodontitis)

📋 What is it?

Gum disease is an infection of the tissues that surround and support your teeth. It ranges from mild gingivitis to severe periodontitis, which can destroy the bone supporting your teeth.

⚠️ Common Causes
  • Plaque buildup along and under the gumline
  • Smoking or tobacco use
  • Genetics and family history
  • Diabetes and other systemic conditions
  • Hormonal changes (pregnancy, menopause)
💊 How We Treat It
  • Scaling & Root Planing: Deep cleaning to remove tartar from beneath the gums
  • Ultrasonic Cleaning: Gentle removal of plaque and tartar
  • Antimicrobial Therapy: Local antibiotics to control infection
  • Regular Maintenance: Ongoing supportive care to prevent recurrence
🩺 Non-Surgical Options Available
🛡️ Prevention

Daily brushing, flossing, and regular dental cleanings are your best defense against gum disease. We'll guide you on proper techniques.

Tooth Sensitivity

📋 What is it?

Tooth sensitivity is a sharp pain or discomfort in your teeth when exposed to hot, cold, sweet, or acidic foods. It occurs when the protective enamel wears down, exposing the sensitive dentin underneath.

⚠️ Common Causes
  • Enamel erosion from acidic foods or aggressive brushing
  • Receding gums (exposes tooth roots)
  • Cracked or chipped teeth
  • Teeth grinding (bruxism)
  • Recent dental work
💊 How We Treat It
  • Fluoride Varnish: Applied to strengthen enamel and reduce sensitivity
  • Desensitizing Agents: Customized treatments to block pain signals
  • Bonding: To cover exposed roots or repair worn enamel
  • Night Guards: For patients who grind their teeth
🔬 Gentle, Effective Relief
🛡️ Prevention

Use a soft-bristled toothbrush, avoid acidic foods, and follow a gentle brushing technique. We'll recommend the best products for your sensitivity.

Oral Cancer

📋 What is it?

Oral cancer refers to cancers that develop in the mouth, lips, tongue, gums, or throat. Early detection is critical for successful treatment and survival.

⚠️ Common Causes
  • Tobacco use (smoking and chewing)
  • Excessive alcohol consumption
  • HPV (human papillomavirus) infection
  • Prolonged sun exposure (lip cancer)
  • Family history of cancer
💊 How We Treat It
  • VELscope® Screening: Advanced technology for early detection
  • Thorough Exams: Regular screenings during routine checkups
  • Biopsy Referral: If suspicious tissues are found, we refer you to a specialist
  • Education: Awareness of warning signs and risk factors
🔬 VELscope® Technology
🛡️ Prevention

Regular screenings, avoiding tobacco, limiting alcohol, and using sun protection for your lips are key prevention strategies.

Bruxism (Teeth Grinding)

📋 What is it?

Bruxism is the involuntary grinding or clenching of teeth, often during sleep. It can cause jaw pain, headaches, tooth wear, and even cracked teeth.

⚠️ Common Causes
  • Stress and anxiety
  • Sleep disorders (sleep apnea)
  • Misaligned or missing teeth
  • Medications (antidepressants, stimulants)
  • Caffeine and alcohol consumption
💊 How We Treat It
  • Night Guards: Custom-fitted guards to protect teeth from grinding
  • Stress Management: Techniques to reduce jaw tension
  • Dental Correction: Addressing misaligned teeth
  • Relaxation Exercises: To reduce muscle tension
😴 Custom-Fitted Night Guards
🛡️ Prevention

Stress reduction, limiting caffeine, and using a night guard can help prevent grinding. We'll help you find the right solution.

Chronic Bad Breath (Halitosis)

📋 What is it?

Chronic bad breath is a persistent unpleasant odor from the mouth. It's often caused by bacteria that produce sulfur compounds, and it can be a sign of underlying health issues.

⚠️ Common Causes
  • Poor oral hygiene and trapped food particles
  • Gum disease or tooth decay
  • Dry mouth (xerostomia)
  • Smoking or tobacco use
  • Medical conditions (sinus infections, diabetes)
💊 How We Treat It
  • Professional Cleanings: Removing plaque and tartar buildup
  • Addressing Underlying Issues: Treating cavities, gum disease, or dry mouth
  • Oral Hygiene Education: Proper brushing and flossing techniques
  • Mouthwash & Products: Recommended specifically for your condition
✅ Fresh Breath Solutions
🛡️ Prevention

Brush twice daily, floss regularly, stay hydrated, and visit us for routine cleanings to keep your breath fresh.
