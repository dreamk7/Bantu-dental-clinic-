Tooth Discoloration

📋 What is it?

Tooth discoloration is the darkening or yellowing of teeth caused by stains on the surface or changes in the tooth's internal structure. It can affect your confidence and smile.

⚠️ Common Causes
  • Frequent consumption of coffee, tea, wine, or dark sodas
  • Smoking or tobacco use
  • Aging and natural wear of enamel
  • Certain medications (tetracycline, antihistamines)
  • Trauma or injury to the tooth
💊 How We Treat It
  • Professional Teeth Whitening: Philips Zoom® in-office whitening — 1 hour
  • Take-Home Whitening Kits: Custom trays for gradual brightening
  • Porcelain Veneers: Ultra-thin shells for severe discoloration
  • Dental Bonding: Composite resin for localized stains
⚡ 1-Hour Whitening
🛡️ Prevention

Rinse your mouth after drinking staining beverages, avoid tobacco, and maintain regular dental cleanings to keep your smile bright.

Chipped or Broken Teeth

📋 What is it?

A chipped or broken tooth is a fracture in the tooth structure caused by trauma, decay, or biting on hard objects. It can affect both function and appearance.

⚠️ Common Causes
  • Sports injuries or accidents
  • Biting on hard foods (ice, nuts, hard candy)
  • Teeth grinding (bruxism)
  • Large existing fillings that weaken the tooth
  • Untreated decay
💊 How We Treat It
  • Dental Bonding: Composite resin for small chips — completed in one visit
  • Porcelain Veneers: For larger chips affecting the front teeth
  • Crowns: For severely broken teeth requiring full coverage
  • Onlays/Inlays: For moderate damage
⏱️ Same-Day Results
🛡️ Prevention

Wear a mouthguard during sports, avoid chewing hard objects, and use a night guard if you grind your teeth.

Gaps Between Teeth

📋 What is it?

Gaps or spaces between teeth (diastema) can occur anywhere in the mouth. They can affect your bite, speech, and the appearance of your smile.

⚠️ Common Causes
  • Genetics — naturally smaller teeth or larger jawbone
  • Missing or impacted teeth
  • Thumb sucking or tongue thrusting habits
  • Gum disease that causes teeth to shift
  • Incorrect swallowing reflex
💊 How We Treat It
  • Porcelain Veneers: Custom shells to close gaps
  • Dental Bonding: Composite resin to build up teeth
  • Invisalign®: Clear aligners to shift teeth together
  • Orthodontic Treatment: Traditional braces for significant gaps
🦷 Invisalign® Gold Provider
🛡️ Prevention

Early orthodontic evaluation in children can prevent gaps from worsening. Good oral hygiene prevents gum disease that can cause shifting.

Crooked or Misaligned Teeth

📋 What is it?

Crooked or misaligned teeth can affect your bite, speech, and smile appearance. They can also make cleaning difficult, increasing the risk of decay and gum disease.

⚠️ Common Causes
  • Genetics and inherited jaw size
  • Childhood habits (thumb sucking, pacifier use)
  • Premature loss of baby teeth
  • Overcrowding of teeth
  • Trauma that shifts teeth
💊 How We Treat It
  • Invisalign®: Virtually invisible clear aligners
  • Porcelain Veneers: For mild misalignment
  • Dental Bonding: For minor adjustments
  • Orthodontics: Traditional braces for complex cases
🥇 Gold Invisalign® Provider
🛡️ Prevention

Regular dental checkups from an early age can catch misalignment early. We'll help you choose the best option for your smile goals.

Worn Enamel

📋 What is it?

Enamel is the protective outer layer of your teeth. When it wears away, teeth become sensitive, discolored, and more prone to decay.

⚠️ Common Causes
  • Acidic foods and drinks (citrus, soda, vinegar)
  • Aggressive brushing with hard bristles
  • Teeth grinding (bruxism)
  • Acid reflux or GERD
  • Dry mouth (reduced saliva protection)
💊 How We Treat It
  • Dental Bonding: To rebuild worn areas
  • Porcelain Veneers: For severely worn front teeth
  • Fluoride Treatments: To strengthen remaining enamel
  • Night Guards: If grinding is the cause
💪 Enamel Strengthening
🛡️ Prevention

Use a soft-bristled brush, avoid acidic foods, drink water after meals, and use a straw for acidic beverages.

Gummy Smile

📋 What is it?

A gummy smile is when excessive gum tissue is visible when you smile. It can make teeth appear shorter and affect the overall balance of your smile.

⚠️ Common Causes
  • Genetics — naturally thick or overgrown gum tissue
  • Teeth eruption issues (teeth appear short)
  • Hyperactive upper lip muscles
  • Jaw positioning
  • Aging that causes gums to recede (opposite effect)
💊 How We Treat It
  • Gum Contouring: Laser reshaping of gum line for a balanced smile
  • Porcelain Veneers: To lengthen teeth and reduce gum visibility
  • Orthodontic Treatment: To correct bite and jaw alignment
  • Botox®: For hyperactive lip muscles (temporary option)
🔬 Laser Precision
🛡️ Prevention

Good oral hygiene keeps gums healthy. We can discuss treatment options to give you the smile you've always wanted.
